Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Wordnook mobile game (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects and how it is used. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

Wordnook is a word puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. Your game progress is stored on your device. The online leaderboards use a random, anonymous player ID created by the App — not your name or any account. Like most free games, we show ads through Google AdMob, which uses a device advertising identifier.

Information we collect

1. Gameplay data (stored on your device). Your level progress, coins, stars, and settings are saved locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Leaderboards and tournaments (anonymous). To run the global leaderboard, the weekly tournament and the daily puzzle race, the App creates a random player ID on your device and sends it to our database (Google Firebase Cloud Firestore) together with your game results: stars, level, streak, weekly star total, and your daily puzzle solve time and number of hints. Your leaderboard name is generated automatically from that ID (for example “Swift42”) — you never type a name, and nothing you enter is uploaded. This ID is separate from the advertising identifier and is not used for advertising or tracking. Leaderboard entries are visible to other players.

3. Usage statistics (anonymous). Once per day that you open the App, we record the same random player ID, the date, the date you first opened the App, and your platform (iOS or Android). We use this only to measure how many players return to the game, so we can tell whether new features help. It is not shared with anyone and not used for advertising.

4. Advertising data (Google AdMob). We use Google AdMob to show ads (rewarded videos and occasional interstitials). AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as a device advertising identifier, general device information, ad-interaction data, and app diagnostics (crash and performance data) to serve and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Tracking. Because this advertising identifier can be combined with data from other apps and websites to show you more relevant ads and to measure advertising, this counts as tracking under Apple’s App Store rules. On iOS we ask for your permission before any tracking happens — see “Your choices” below.

We do not collect or transmit any other personal information, and there is no account or login.

How we use information

  • To run the game (save your progress locally on your device).
  • To show leaderboards, run the weekly tournament and daily race, and pay tournament prizes (in-game coins).
  • To measure, in aggregate, how many players come back to the game.
  • To display advertising that keeps the App free.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) — stores leaderboard results and usage statistics. firebase.google.com/support/privacy

These services have their own privacy policies governing their handling of data.

Your choices

  • iOS — App Tracking Transparency: The first time you open the App, iOS asks whether Wordnook may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, the App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking and you will see non-personalized ads instead. The game remains fully playable either way — nothing is locked behind allowing tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android — advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Local data: Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device.
  • Leaderboard and usage data: Email us your leaderboard name (shown on the Leaderboard screen) and we will delete the records linked to it.

Children’s privacy

Wordnook is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Support

Need help with Wordnook, found a bug, or want your data removed? Email waleed@uniqlearn.co and we will get back to you.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on your device, so it does not transfer between devices and is lost if you uninstall the App.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

See Your choices in the privacy policy above — you can decline tracking on iOS, or reset your advertising ID on Android.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes — all 3,000 levels work offline. Only the ads and the leaderboards need a connection.
